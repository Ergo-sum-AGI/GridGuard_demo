    Welcome to GridGuard™
    Select a scenario above and press Play. GridGuard will analyze the incoming frequency data in real time and predict grid instability minutes before it becomes catastrophic — giving operators time to act.
    📖 What does it all mean?
    Frequency (Hz)
    Power grids run at exactly 50 Hz (EU) or 60 Hz (US). If too many generators trip or too much load is added, the frequency drops — like a car engine under stress. Below 47.5 Hz, automatic protection systems shut everything down.
    RoCoF (Rate of Change of Frequency)
    How fast the frequency is falling — in Hz per second. A slow drift is manageable. A rapid plunge (>0.5 Hz/s) means a major generator just failed and a cascade is starting.
    Cascade Failure
    When one generator fails, others become overloaded and trip too — like dominoes. Within seconds, a whole region can go dark. GridGuard detects the mathematical signature of this process before it unfolds.
    Dunkelflaute
    German: "Dark doldrums." When wind and solar produce nothing for days, and no fossil backup remains, grid frequency slowly starves. Without the rotational inertia of spinning turbines, the grid has no buffer — collapse can be total and fast.
